# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy focused on a concentrated mid-game push, reinforced with Stimpack research and Medivac support to sustain the breakthrough. Add Marauders to the attack force to counter heavy armored compositions, ensuring the first attack can break through Thors and Siege Tanks. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with upgraded Marines and Marauders, and reinforce the attack with the same composition plus Medivacs.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine, Marauder, Siege Tank, and Medivac production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack begins. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, and 1 Starport. Continuously produce Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs while maintaining the resources and supply required for all unit types.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 2 Barracks with Tech Labs, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield, Stimpack, and Concussive Shells. Keep the tech path on Barracks, Factories, Starport, Combat Shield, Stimpack, and Concussive Shells for the Marine-Marauder-Siege Tank core.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, 4 completed and living Medivacs, and 10 completed and living Marauders are available. All four thresholds must be satisfied. These thresholds only open the offensive; Marine, Marauder, Siege Tank, and Medivac production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 45 Marines, 10 Siege Tanks, 4 Medivacs, and 10 Marauders, and concentrate them before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 60 Marines, 15 Marauders, 14 Siege Tanks, 8 Medivacs, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine, Marauder, Siege Tank, and Medivac production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
