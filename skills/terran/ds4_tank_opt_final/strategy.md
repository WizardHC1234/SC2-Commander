# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy with Viking anti-air support and a Raven for detection, focused on a concentrated mid-game push. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with Marines, and reinforce the attack with the same composition plus Vikings and a Raven to counter enemy air and burrowed threats. The attack launches only when the full required force is gathered at the staging zone, ensuring maximum combat power at contact. Research Stimpack in parallel with Combat Shield before the first commitment to increase Marine damage output without delaying the attack.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine, Siege Tank, Viking, and Raven production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack begins. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, and 1 Starport. Continuously produce Marines, Siege Tanks, and Vikings, and maintain one Raven, while maintaining the resources and supply required for all unit types.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, both Factories with Tech Labs, and the Starport with a Tech Lab (to enable Raven production). Research Combat Shield and Stimpack from the Barracks Tech Lab, running both upgrades in parallel before the first attack. Keep the tech path on Barracks, Factories, Starport, Combat Shield, Stimpack, and the Starport Tech Lab for the Marine-Siege Tank-Viking-Raven core.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection beyond the Raven. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, Vikings, and the Raven concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. The attack will not launch until the entire required force is present at that staging zone.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack only when all of the following are true: at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, 4 completed and living Vikings, and 1 completed and living Raven are available, AND the entire required force is gathered at the staging zone. These thresholds only open the offensive; production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, Vikings, and replacement Ravens to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to the attack gate (including the Raven), and concentrate them at the staging zone before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 88 Marines, 16 Siege Tanks, 8 Vikings, 1 Raven, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine, Siege Tank, Viking, and Raven production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Replace combat losses, including the Raven, until all enemy structures are destroyed.
