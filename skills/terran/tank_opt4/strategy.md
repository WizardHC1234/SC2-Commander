# Summary

A Marine-Siege Tank-Medivac strategy built around a concentrated mid-game power spike. Establish the core economy and upgrades, move the assembled army into a forward staging position, and commit before prolonged holding allows the opponent to consolidate a larger economy and stronger defensive line.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine, Siege Tank, and Medivac production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center. Start the third Command Center at approximately 550-600 seconds, before or alongside the main attack, so reinforcement production can continue if the first engagement becomes prolonged.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. Continuously produce Marines and Siege Tanks while maintaining the resources and supply required for both unit types. After the 2nd Factory Tech Lab is complete, build a Starport with Tech Lab and produce 2-3 Medivacs before the main attack; continue Marine, Siege Tank, and Medivac production during the attack and reinforcement phase.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield, then Stimpack on the Barracks Tech Lab. After the Starport is started, build an Engineering Bay and research Infantry Weapons 1 followed by Infantry Armor 1 when resources allow without stopping core unit production. Keep the technology path focused on the Marine-Siege Tank-Medivac core.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the intended first objective when its information is missing or stale. After an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands. Request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. A scan should improve the attack decision, but missing scan information must not keep a ready army holding beyond the time-bounded commitment conditions below.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Once the minimum force is assembled and the game approaches 580 seconds, move the concentrated army to a forward staging zone so it can begin the assault promptly when a commitment condition is met.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack when any one of the following conditions is satisfied: (1) at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, and 2 completed and living Medivacs are concentrated, with the intended objective updated by a recent scan or scout; (2) the game has reached 600 seconds, at least 45 Marines, 8 Siege Tanks, and 2 Medivacs are concentrated, and Combat Shield, Stimpack, Infantry Weapons 1, and Infantry Armor 1 are complete; or (3) the game has reached 650 seconds and at least 45 Marines, 8 Siege Tanks, and 2 Medivacs are concentrated. Request a scan when useful, but do not continue holding solely for vision after condition (2) or (3) is met. Re-evaluate the attack at 600 and 650 seconds instead of escalating the wake condition toward the Ultimate Goal counts.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone. Rebuild to at least 45 Marines, 8 Siege Tanks, and 2 Medivacs, concentrate the force, and use the same information and commitment rules before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 75 Marines, 14 Siege Tanks, 4 Medivacs, and 44 SCVs while respecting the 200-supply limit. These counts guide reinforcement after the first commitment; they must not delay an attack once one of the Main Attack Gate conditions is satisfied. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
