# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank timing strategy that builds a concentrated mid-game force, completes Combat Shield, Stimpack, and Vehicle Weapons 1, and attacks the enemy main at a 45-Marine/10-Siege-Tank gate. If the first push does not end the game, the strategy expands to four bases, grows the economy to 60 SCVs, and rebuilds a materially larger Marine/Siege Tank force before attacking again. The end-state army is 80 Marines, 20 Siege Tanks, and 60 SCVs, filling the supply cap with the same core composition.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Grow toward 44 living SCVs and replace losses before the first attack, but do not let Marine scaling delay the second Command Center or the Factory-Siege Tank production chain. After the third Command Center is active, keep training SCVs toward 60 and replace losses to that target through the late game.

* Expansion: Treat the second Command Center as a mandatory opening priority and keep pursuing it until two active mineral-bearing bases are established. Once living SCV count reaches 40, start the third Command Center at a safe expansion, but keep the pre-attack production and research priorities ahead of its construction. Start the fourth Command Center immediately after the first assault order is issued or after an auto-retreat from the first assault. Morph every new Command Center to an Orbital Command.

* Gas: Build exactly 2 Refineries on the first base. Until the second Command Center becomes active, the Refinery target is 2; afterward raise the target to a total of 4.

* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. Continuously produce Marines and Siege Tanks while maintaining the resources and supply required for both unit types. Keep producing from these structures during the recovery and second-push phase.

* Pre-Attack Production Priority: Before the first planned attack, prevent immediate supply blocks first, then prioritize the second Command Center, missing Factories and Factory Tech Labs, continuous Siege Tank production, and, once Combat Shield is underway, one Armory and the Stimpack and Vehicle Weapons 1 researches ahead of additional Marine and SCV spending. Keep continuous Siege Tank production while these upgrades are underway. The third Command Center is requested at 40 SCVs but must not delay these pre-attack items.

* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield at the Barracks Tech Lab, then Stimpack after it finishes. After both Factories and their Tech Labs are active, build one Armory and research Vehicle Weapons 1 at it. Stimpack and Vehicle Weapons 1 must both complete before the first assault crosses the midpoint.

* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.

* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.

* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines and Siege Tanks concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete and both Stimpack and Vehicle Weapons 1 are complete.

* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, and both Stimpack and Vehicle Weapons 1 upgrades are complete. All conditions must be satisfied. These thresholds only open the first offensive; Marine and Siege Tank production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.

* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.

* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines and Siege Tanks to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.

* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone and start the fourth Command Center if it is not already under way. Do not order another assault until at least 75 completed and living Marines and 15 completed and living Siege Tanks are concentrated with the main force. Never leave again with only 45 Marines and 10 Siege Tanks. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.

* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward 80 Marines, 20 Siege Tanks, and 60 SCVs, filling the 200-supply limit with the Marine/Siege Tank core and the expanded mining economy. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine and Siege Tank production toward these counts. Replace combat and worker losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
