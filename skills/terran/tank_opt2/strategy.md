# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy focused on a concentrated mid-game push. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with Marines, and reinforce the attack with the same composition.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine and Siege Tank production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack begins. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. Build the 2nd Factory as early as mineral income allows after the 1st Factory Tech Lab is complete, with a target to begin construction before 250s and complete by 300s. Build a Tech Lab on each Factory immediately upon completion. When 2 Factory Tech Labs are complete and gas exceeds 200, produce Siege Tanks at equal or higher priority than Marines until 6 living Siege Tanks are reached. After 6 Siege Tanks are living, add train_marauder to reach 8 living Marauders, prioritized above additional Marines. After the 2nd Factory Tech Lab is complete and at least 6 Siege Tanks are living, build an Engineering Bay when minerals are at least 175. After Engineering Bay begins, when minerals exceed 400 and gas exceeds 100, build 2 Starports. Immediately build a Tech Lab on the first Starport and a Reactor on the second Starport. From the Starport Reactor, produce Medivacs to reach 4 living Medivacs. From the Starport Tech Lab, produce Vikings to reach 2 living Vikings. Continuously produce Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, Medivacs, and Vikings from all available infrastructure while maintaining the resources and supply required for all unit types. After the attack gate is satisfied, maintain equal production priority for all unit types toward Ultimate Goal counts.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors and 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab. Equip both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield first on the Barracks Tech Lab. After Combat Shield completes, research Stimpack on the same Barracks Tech Lab when gas is at least 150 and Siege Tank count is at least 4. After the 2nd Factory Tech Lab is complete and at least 6 Siege Tanks are living, build an Engineering Bay when minerals are at least 175 and the 2nd Factory Tech Lab has been complete for at least 30 seconds. Research Infantry Weapons 1 on the Engineering Bay when minerals are at least 150 and Infantry Armor 1 when minerals are at least 150 after Infantry Weapons 1 completes. Keep the core tech path focused on Barracks, Factories, Starport, Combat Shield, Stimpack, and the Marine-Marauder-Siege Tank package with Medivac and Viking support.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed. Identify mech-bio indicators during scouting: count Factories and Armories, and note any observed HellionTank, Thor, or WidowMine. Report these observations to inform the attack gate qualifier.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands. Before assaulting into enemy territory where burrowed Widow Mines, Raven Auto-Turrets, or cloaked units are possible, request a Scanner Sweep if Orbital energy is at least 50 and fewer than 2 Vikings are present in the assaulting force. If energy is below 50 and fewer than 2 Vikings are present, hold the assault and wait for energy or 2 Viking arrival. Use Scanner Sweep for fog clearing when ground scouting is unsafe even if Vikings are present. Do not assault without either 2 Vikings or a completed Scanner Sweep.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, Medivacs, and Vikings concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 45 completed and living Marines and 10 completed and living Siege Tanks are available. Both unit thresholds must be satisfied. If enemy Factory count is 3 or higher, or enemy Armory count is 1 or higher, or enemy HellionTank, Thor, or WidowMine has been observed, also require 8 completed and living Marauders, 4 completed and living Medivacs, and 2 completed and living Vikings before assault. If the mech-bio qualifier applies and Viking count is below 2, request a Scanner Sweep if Orbital energy is at least 50 and hold the assault until scan completes; if energy is below 50, hold the assault until energy reaches 50 or Viking count reaches 2. These thresholds only open the offensive; unit production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Marauders, Siege Tanks, Medivacs, and Vikings to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild until the main attack gate is satisfied, and concentrate them before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 40 Marines, 15 Siege Tanks, 8 Marauders, 4 Medivacs, 2 Vikings, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain production of Marauders, Siege Tanks, Medivacs, and Vikings toward their Ultimate Goal counts. Reduce Marine production to maintain approximately 40 living Marines, replacing only combat losses. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
