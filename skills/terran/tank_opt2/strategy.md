# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy focused on a concentrated mid-game push. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with Marines, and reinforce the attack with the same composition.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine and Siege Tank production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack begins. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. Continuously produce Marines and Siege Tanks while maintaining the resources and supply required for both unit types. After the 2nd Factory Tech Lab is complete, build a Starport with Tech Lab and produce 2-3 Medivacs before the main attack; continue Medivac production as supply allows during the attack and reinforcement phase.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield, then Stimpack on the Barracks Tech Lab. After the Starport is started, build an Engineering Bay and research Infantry Weapons 1 followed by Infantry Armor 1 when minerals allow without delaying Siege Tank or Medivac production. Keep the tech path focused on Marine-Siege Tank core support.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands. Request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. When the attack gate is satisfied but vision of the intended objective is missing, request a scan and hold the push in the staging zone until the sweep completes or energy is unavailable; if energy is unavailable, commit only if a living SCV scout is already updating the target zone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete, including the required Medivac support and confirmed vision of the objective.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, and 2-3 completed and living Medivacs are available, and vision of the intended objective is confirmed by a recent scan or scout. If the unit thresholds are met but vision is missing, request a Scanner Sweep when Orbital energy is available and hold in the staging zone; if energy is unavailable, commit only with active scouting present. Both unit thresholds and vision must be satisfied to open the offensive. These thresholds only open the offensive; Marine, Siege Tank, and Medivac production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, and Medivacs to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 45 Marines, 10 Siege Tanks, and 2 Medivacs, and concentrate them before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 90 Marines, 18 Siege Tanks, 4 Medivacs, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine, Siege Tank, and Medivac production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
