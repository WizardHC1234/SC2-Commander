# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy focused on an earlier mid-game push, enhanced with Liberator and Raven support and Vehicle Weapons Level 1 to hit before the enemy tech wall forms. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with Marines, Liberators, and Ravens, and reinforce the attack with the same composition.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine, Siege Tank, Liberator, and Raven production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack begins. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, and 1 Starport. Continuously produce Marines, Siege Tanks, Liberators, and Ravens while maintaining the resources and supply required for all unit types.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, both Factories with Tech Labs, and the Starport with a Tech Lab. Build an Armory. Research Combat Shield and Vehicle Weapons Level 1. Keep the tech path on Barracks, Factories, Starport, Armory, Combat Shield, and Vehicle Weapons Level 1 for the Marine-Siege Tank-Liberator-Raven core.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, Liberators, and Ravens concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 35 completed and living Marines, 8 completed and living Siege Tanks, 2 completed and living Liberators, and 2 completed and living Ravens are available, and Vehicle Weapons Level 1 is completed. All unit thresholds and the upgrade must be satisfied. These thresholds only open the offensive; Marine, Siege Tank, Liberator, and Raven production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, Liberators, and Ravens to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 35 Marines, 8 Siege Tanks, 2 Liberators, and 2 Ravens, and concentrate them before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 80 Marines, 16 Siege Tanks, 4 Liberators, 4 Ravens, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine, Siege Tank, Liberator, and Raven production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
