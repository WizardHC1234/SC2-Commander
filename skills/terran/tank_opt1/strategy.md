# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank mid-game push that expands to a third base and 55 SCVs before attacking, researches Combat Shield and Stimpack, and commits only after a pre-attack scan. The concentrated 45-Marine/10-Siege Tank force is followed by continuous Marine/Tank production, and a failed first push is followed by a stronger 60/15 re-engagement instead of a weak repeat.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and build an early second Command Center. Produce SCVs continuously to 55 living SCVs before the main attack; after the attack begins, replace SCV losses toward the same count. Keep supply depots ahead of SCV and army production.
* Expansion: Start the third Command Center before the main attack begins, as soon as 44 living SCVs, 3 Barracks, and 2 Factories are complete and at least 30 living Marines exist. Do not wait for the attack to start. Begin further expansions only after the attack is underway.
* Production: Maintain absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. With 2 Reactor Barracks, 1 Tech Lab Barracks, and 2 Tech Lab Factories, keep Marines and Siege Tanks in continuous production. Prioritize the pre-attack force and the third Command Center over optional upgrades, while preserving enough resources for the next wave.
* Technology: Research Combat Shield, then immediately research Stimpack in the same Barracks Tech Lab. Do not launch the main attack until Stimpack is complete.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main when the route is reasonably safe. When 40 living Marines and 8 living Siege Tanks are in the main force, request a Scanner Sweep on the enemy main and, if no SCV scout is alive, send one SCV toward the enemy natural. After an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Convert the first Command Centers to Orbital Commands early enough to bank the energy for the pre-attack sweep. Use later Scanner Sweeps when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Do not launch the first attack until the pre-attack sweep has completed or its 60-second fallback has expired.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Keep available Marines and Siege Tanks concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack only when all of the following are true: at least 55 living SCVs, at least 45 completed and living Marines in the main force, at least 10 completed and living Siege Tanks in the main force, Stimpack is complete, and the pre-attack sweep has completed or its 60-second fallback has expired. These thresholds only open the offensive; the Ultimate Goal counts below remain the production targets.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines and Siege Tanks to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone. Rebuild to at least 60 living Marines and 15 living Siege Tanks in the main force, ensure Stimpack is complete and the third Command Center is at least under construction, and concentrate before attacking again. Do not re-engage at the original 45/10 gate after a failed attack. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 90 Marines, 18 Siege Tanks, and 55 SCVs to stay within the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine and Siege Tank production toward those counts, replace combat losses, and continue until all enemy structures are destroyed.
