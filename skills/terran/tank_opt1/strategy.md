# Summary

Two-base Terran Marine-Siege Tank timing push with a small Viking escort and Stimpack before the first offensive. Establish four refineries and roughly 44 SCVs on two bases, build three Barracks (two Reactor, one Tech Lab) and two Factories (Tech Labs), add one Engineering Bay and one Reactored Starport, then attack with a concentrated force of 60 Marines, 12 Siege Tanks, and 4 Vikings once Combat Shield and Stimpack are complete. Reinforce with the same core, keep scouting and scanning the intended target, and continue producing toward 88 Marines, 20 Siege Tanks, 4 Vikings, and 44 SCVs until all enemy structures are destroyed.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build an early natural Command Center, take both gas geysers at each of the first two bases for 4 total Refineries, and grow to roughly 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marines, Siege Tanks, and the small Viking escort.
* Expansion: Remain on two bases until the first attack begins; start the third Command Center after that attack has begun.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, 1 Engineering Bay, and 1 Starport. Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, both Factories with Tech Labs, and the Starport with a Reactor. Keep Marines and Siege Tanks producing continuously, and build up to 4 Vikings once the Starport is online.
* Technology: Ensure Combat Shield is complete, then research Stimpack at the Barracks Tech Lab; do not launch the first attack before Stimpack is complete. From the Engineering Bay, research Infantry Weapons Level 1 and Infantry Armor Level 1 as soon as practical, with higher priority once the first push begins so later reinforcements benefit.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main when the route is reasonably safe. Before the attack gate, if the enemy natural or intended first objective is missing or stale, send one more worker scout to that area; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands. Request a Scanner Sweep on the enemy main or intended objective just before launching if that area is not visible; if the objective is already visible, scan in parallel with the ready attack. Also use scans when ground scouting is unsafe or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, and Vikings concentrated at one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch only after that force is gathered, the required upgrades are complete, and the intended objective is known well enough to attack.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack when at least 60 completed and living Marines, 12 completed and living Siege Tanks, and 4 completed and living Vikings are available, Combat Shield and Stimpack are complete, and the force is concentrated. These thresholds open the offensive; production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Prefer the enemy main when it is visible and is not clearly more fortified than a forward base. If the pre-attack scan shows a heavily defended main, assault the enemy natural or the most forward known enemy base instead, then continue to the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing while it can make progress; anchor fights with Siege Tank range. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, and Vikings to the same objective, merging into the main force rather than starting a separate route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 60 Marines, 12 Siege Tanks, and 4 Vikings, finish any missing Infantry upgrades, and concentrate before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward roughly 88 Marines, 20 Siege Tanks, 4 Vikings, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine, Siege Tank, and Viking production toward those counts. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
