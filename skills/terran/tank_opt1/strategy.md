# Summary
A two-base Marine-Siege Tank timing push with a small Starport support line. Establish a moderate economy on two bases with full gas, build 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, and a Starport with Tech Lab, research Stimpack, Combat Shield, and +1 Infantry Weapons and Armor, then launch a concentrated attack on the enemy main with Marines, Siege Tanks, Vikings, and Ravens. Reinforce the same objective with the same composition, and start a third base when the attack gate is met or minerals begin to deplete.

# Details
* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine, Siege Tank, Viking, and Raven production.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until the main attack gate is met or the main base mineral patches fall below roughly 1000 remaining minerals, whichever comes first; then start the third Command Center. When minerals are low, build the third Command Center ahead of production beyond the core build so income does not collapse, while keeping the core Marine-Tank-Viking-Raven build funded.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks, 2 Factories, and 1 Starport with Tech Lab. Continuously produce Marines and Siege Tanks, and before the main attack gate produce 4 Vikings and 2 Ravens from the Starport; after the gate, replace losses toward those same support counts so the anti-air and detection line stays with the army. Keep supply ahead of demand so the staged force completes without capping.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Build 1 Engineering Bay after the Factories are complete. Research Combat Shield and Stimpack from the Barracks Tech Lab and +1 Infantry Weapons and +1 Infantry Armor from the Engineering Bay before the main attack gate.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Send a second SCV scout when the natural base is saturated to check the enemy natural or nearest forward expansion for expansion and tech choices. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Before committing the attack, scan the intended objective if its composition or defenses are unseen; if the scan reveals a fortified position or a defending force the staged army cannot beat, shift the objective to a weaker enemy base or expansion rather than attacking blind.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines, Siege Tanks, Vikings, and Ravens concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete and the required upgrades are finished.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack only when at least 45 completed and living Marines, 10 completed and living Siege Tanks, 4 completed and living Vikings, and 2 completed and living Ravens are available and Combat Shield, Stimpack, +1 Infantry Weapons, and +1 Infantry Armor are complete. These thresholds only open the offensive; Marine, Siege Tank, Viking, and Raven production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid, provided the latest scan or scout does not show it heavily fortified. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure; if the main proves too heavily defended, attack the nearest weaker enemy base or expansion before returning to the main.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines, Siege Tanks, Vikings, and Ravens to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to the same attack gate with upgrades intact, and scan or scout the intended objective before re-engaging. Do not immediately re-attack the same fortified objective; attack the nearest weaker base or expansion when the scan shows the main is still too strong. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 84 Marines, 18 Siege Tanks, 4 Vikings, 2 Ravens, and 44 SCVs while staying under the maximum supply, replacing losses until all enemy structures are destroyed. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine and Siege Tank production toward those Ultimate Goal counts.
