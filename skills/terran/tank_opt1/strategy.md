# Summary

A two-base Marine-Siege Tank strategy focused on a concentrated mid-game push. Establish a moderate economy, protect the Tank core with Marines, and reinforce the attack with the same composition.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Build a total of 4 Refineries by taking both gas geysers at each of the first two bases, and grow to approximately 44 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while preserving resources for Marine and Siege Tank production. Prioritize gas income to support continuous Siege Tank production once 2 Factory Tech Labs are operational.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center and remain on two bases until at least 10 completed and living Siege Tanks are available. Start the third Command Center after the attack has begun or after the 10 Siege Tank threshold is reached, whichever comes later.
* Production: Reach absolute counts of 3 Barracks and 2 Factories. Build the 2nd Factory and both Factory Tech Labs as early as feasible, targeting completion before 300 seconds. Once 2 Factory Tech Labs are complete, prioritize train_siege_tank before or equal to train_marine whenever gas exceeds 200 and living Siege Tank count is below 10. Continuously produce Marines and Siege Tanks while maintaining the resources and supply required for both unit types.
* Technology: Equip 2 Barracks with Reactors, 1 Barracks with a Tech Lab, and both Factories with Tech Labs. Research Combat Shield first. After Combat Shield completes, research Stimpack at the Barracks Tech Lab when it would otherwise be idle, without interrupting Siege Tank production. After 2 Factory Tech Labs are complete and Siege Tank production is active, build an Engineering Bay and research Infantry Weapons 1 and Infantry Armor 1 using excess minerals, queuing these only when they do not delay unit production. Keep the tech path focused on Barracks, Factories, and Combat Shield for the Marine-Siege Tank core.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Before the planned attack, update the enemy natural or intended first objective when its information is missing or stale; after an objective is cleared, scout the next relevant enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when needed.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision affects the attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. If Orbital energy is available and vision is missing for the planned assault, request the scan and hold the push until the scan completes and the result is observed; if energy is unavailable, apply the same fallback of holding in the pre-attack staging zone until vision is obtained through other means. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack when energy permits rather than delaying solely for vision.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines and Siege Tanks concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. If enemy pressure arrives before 10 Siege Tanks are completed, concentrate forces defensively at the natural or main base choke rather than staging forward. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 45 completed and living Marines and 10 completed and living Siege Tanks are available. Both unit thresholds must be satisfied. If scan energy is available and vision of the target is missing, request a Scanner Sweep and hold until the result is observed; if scan energy is unavailable, hold in the staging zone until scouting or other vision is obtained. These thresholds only open the offensive; Marine and Siege Tank production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the concentrated force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines and Siege Tanks to the same objective instead of starting a separate attack route.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the force can no longer make progress against a superior defense, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 45 Marines and 10 Siege Tanks, and concentrate them before attacking again. During recovery, maintain the same production priority: prioritize Siege Tanks when 2 Factory Tech Labs are operational, gas exceeds 200, and tank count is below 10. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Continue toward approximately 96 Marines, 20 Siege Tanks, and 44 SCVs to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine and Siege Tank production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Replace combat losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
