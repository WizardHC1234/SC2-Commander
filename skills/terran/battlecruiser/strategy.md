# Summary

A defensive late-game strategy that uses Marines and Siege Tanks to survive the early game before transitioning into a concentrated Tank-Thor-Battlecruiser force supported by a gas-rich economy.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Grow to approximately 50 living SCVs and replace lost SCVs as needed. Reach 2 Refineries for the initial Siege Tank and advanced technology transition, then add Refineries gradually as additional bases become available. Operate on 6 Refineries during the main technology and production buildup, increasing to a maximum of 8 when continuous Battlecruiser production and core-unit replacement make gas the limiting resource.
* Expansion: Build an early second Command Center, expand to 3 bases after stabilizing the opening defense, and start the fourth Command Center after Battlecruiser production has begun when additional mineral income, gas access, or Orbital utility is needed.
* Production: Use a single Barracks for early Marines and as the Factory prerequisite. Reach absolute counts of 2 Factories and 2 Starports. Produce Siege Tanks and Thors from the Factories and continuously produce Battlecruisers from the Starports. Continue Marine production from the Barracks only as needed to maintain the intended support count.
* Technology: Equip both Factories and both Starports with Tech Labs. Build an Armory to unlock Thors and a Fusion Core to unlock Battlecruisers, then research Yamato Cannon before the main heavy-force attack.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. During the technology buildup, sequentially update relevant enemy bases and neutral mineral expansions when needed, and refresh the intended first objective before the heavy-force attack if its information is missing or stale.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep for stale information that affects the heavy-force attack, when necessary ground scouting is unsafe, or for urgent detection. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available ground and air combat units concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 6 completed and living Battlecruisers, 4 completed and living Thors, and 6 completed and living Siege Tanks are available. All three unit thresholds must be satisfied. These thresholds only open the offensive; core-unit production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the nearest known enemy expansion first and maintain this objective until it is cleared, then continue to the enemy main base. If no expansion is known, attack the enemy main base.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the Battlecruisers, Thors, and Siege Tanks committed to the same current objective while they can make progress. Send newly completed core units to that objective instead of starting isolated attacks.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the heavy force can no longer make progress in an unfavorable engagement, withdraw the survivors to a safe owned zone, rebuild the attack-gate composition, and concentrate it before attacking again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Establish a core force of approximately 12 Battlecruisers, 4 Thors, 8 Siege Tanks, and 12 Marines while maintaining approximately 50 SCVs. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise production toward those Ultimate Goal counts. Prioritize additional Battlecruisers and replace lost core units until reaching 200 supply and destroying all enemy structures.

# Resource Costs

* SCV: 50 minerals, 0 gas, 1 supply. Produced from a Command Center.
* Marine: 50 minerals, 0 gas, 1 supply. Produced from a Barracks.
* Siege Tank: 150 minerals, 125 gas, 3 supply. Produced from a Factory with a Factory Tech Lab.
* Thor: 300 minerals, 200 gas, 6 supply. Produced from a Factory with a Factory Tech Lab and requires an Armory.
* Battlecruiser: 400 minerals, 300 gas, 6 supply. Produced from a Starport with a Starport Tech Lab and requires a Fusion Core.
* Supply Depot: 100 minerals, 0 gas. Provides 8 supply.
* Refinery: 75 minerals, 0 gas. Collects gas.
* Barracks: 150 minerals, 0 gas. Requires a completed Supply Depot.
* Factory: 150 minerals, 100 gas. Requires a Barracks.
* Starport: 150 minerals, 100 gas. Requires a Factory.
* Factory Tech Lab: 50 minerals, 25 gas. Attached to a completed Factory and required for Siege Tanks and Thors.
* Starport Tech Lab: 50 minerals, 25 gas. Attached to a completed Starport and required for Battlecruisers.
* Armory: 150 minerals, 100 gas. Requires a Factory and unlocks Thors.
* Fusion Core: 150 minerals, 150 gas. Requires a Starport and unlocks Battlecruiser production.
* Yamato Cannon: 150 minerals, 150 gas. Researched at the Fusion Core.
* Command Center: 400 minerals, 0 gas. Produces SCVs.
* Orbital Command: 150 minerals, 0 gas. Requires a Barracks.
* Scanner Sweep: 50 Orbital energy. Requires an Orbital Command and provides temporary vision and detection.
