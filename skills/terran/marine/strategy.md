# Summary

A one-base Marine rush that sacrifices gas, advanced technology, and expansion for rapid infantry production. Attack as soon as the initial Marine threshold is ready, then maintain pressure through continuous reinforcement.

# Details

* Opening and Economy: Use a mineral-only economy, build 0 Refineries, and grow to approximately 20 living SCVs. Replace lost SCVs as needed while prioritizing continuous Marine production.
* Expansion: Remain on one Command Center for the planned rush and invest minerals into Barracks and Marines.
* Production: Build a total of 6 Barracks and continuously produce Marines from every available production queue while supply and resources permit.
* Technology: Stay on a Barracks-only mineral path: no production add-ons and no unit upgrades; keep spending on Barracks and Marines.
* Scouting: Send one early SCV to scout the enemy main base when the route is reasonably safe. Keep the Marine rush on schedule; after the enemy main is cleared, scout a relevant remaining enemy base or neutral mineral expansion only when it helps locate surviving structures.
* Scans: Upgrade useful Command Centers to Orbital Commands and request a Scanner Sweep when missing vision prevents continued pressure, when a required ground-scout route is unsafe, or when cloaked or burrowed enemies require detection. Prefer scanning in parallel with a ready attack rather than holding the push for vision alone.
* Pre-Attack Army Posture: Before the attack gate is satisfied, keep available Marines concentrated in one safe staging zone near the forward route. Launch the planned attack only after that force is gathered and complete.
* Main Attack Gate: Begin the planned attack as soon as at least 20 completed and living Marines are available. Marine count is the only planned attack threshold. This threshold only opens the offensive; Marine production targets remain the Ultimate Goal counts below.
* Attack Objective: Attack the enemy main base directly and maintain this objective while it remains valid. After it is cleared, continue toward the nearest known enemy base or structure.
* Engagement and Reinforcement: After the planned attack begins, keep the forward force advancing toward the current objective while it can make progress. Send newly completed Marines to the same objective instead of recalling the forward force or waiting at home.
* Recovery and Cleanup: If the forward force can no longer make progress and its strength has fallen below the 20-Marine attack gate, withdraw the surviving Marines to a safe owned zone, rebuild to 20 Marines, and attack again. Search remaining expansions after known enemy bases are destroyed.
* Ultimate Goal: Maintain approximately 20 SCVs and continue toward 180 Marines to fill the 200-supply limit. After the attack gate is met, retain or raise Marine production toward that Ultimate Goal count. Replace Marine losses until all enemy structures are destroyed.
